5. College Terminology

Semester
A half-year academic term, usually about six months long.
Attendance
The record of how many classes a student has attended.
Syllabus
The list of topics a student must study in a course.
Credit
A unit that measures the weight of a course toward a degree.
Viva
An oral exam where a student answers questions asked by examiners.
